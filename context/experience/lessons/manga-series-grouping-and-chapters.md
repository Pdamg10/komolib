# Lesson: Unified Manga/Manhwa/Comic Chapter-Pack Grouping & Structured Info Card

## Context
Downloaded manga, manhwa, manhua, and comics typically arrive split across multiple PDF/CBZ/CBR packs of ~10 chapters each (e.g., `0-10`, `11-21`, `Caps 22-31`), cluttering the shelf with dozens of separate entries and making chapter order, duplicates, and available chapter counts hard to track. Standard books (`kind === 'book'`) and audiobooks (`kind === 'audiobook'`) must preserve their exact existing structure.

## Design & Invariants
1. **Virtual/Logical Grouping over Underlying `BookItem` Files:**
   - Never merge multi-GB PDFs/CBZs into a single binary blob (would OOM low-end Android WebViews and break Drive sync).
   - Keep each downloaded pack as a `BookItem` enriched with optional chapter-pack metadata (`seriesName`, `chapterStart`, `chapterEnd`, `originTag`, `seriesStatus`, `fansubs`, `genres`, `totalChaptersExpected`) in `src/types/library.ts`.
   - Because `BookItem` syncs wholesale across IndexedDB, Google Drive (`komolib-state.json`), Cloudflare Workers D1, and Express, series metadata syncs automatically between PC and APK with zero backend schema changes.
2. **Strict Visual-Only Scope (`isVisualSeriesKind`):**
   - `groupLibraryForDisplay()` in `src/utils/mangaSeriesUtils.ts` only groups `manga`, `manhwa`, and `comic` items. Books and audiobooks pass through untouched.
   - Numeric range auto-classification in `src/parsers/index.ts` (`guessContentKind`) and `src/context/LibraryContext.tsx` (`importFiles`, `importNativeFolderBooks`) only inspects `pdf`, `cbz`, `cbr`, and `images` (guarding against year ranges like `2020-2025`), never reclassifying EPUB/MOBI/DOCX/TXT/audio files.
3. **Deduplication, Ordering & Filter Integrity:**
   - `src/components/BookShelf.tsx` groups `baseFiltered` into `ShelfDisplayItem[]` **before** applying reading-status filters (`Todos`, `Sin leer`, `Leyendo`, `Finalizados`) and sorting, so finishing chapters `0-10` while reading `11-21` keeps the whole series intact under `Leyendo`.
   - Exact same `(seriesKey + chapterStart + chapterEnd)` ranges are blocked on import and deduplicated on display (keeping the highest-quality copy and surfacing a 1-click cleanup button in `src/components/MangaSeriesModal.tsx`).
4. **1-Click Telegram Card Parsing (`NFKD` Normalization):**
   - `parseTelegramSeriesCard()` normalizes mathematical Unicode bold labels (e.g., `𝐍𝐨𝐦𝐛𝐫𝐞` → `Nombre`) via `.normalize('NFKD')`, extracting `Nombre`, `Tipo`, `Género`, `Fansubs`, `Estado`, `Capítulos`, and `Sinopsis` in one click and applying them across all files in the series via `updateSeriesBooks()`.
5. **In-Reader Chapter Switching:**
   - `src/components/readers/ReaderShell.tsx` uses `getSeriesNavigationForBook()` to render previous/next chapter-pack navigation buttons on Desktop, Tablet, and Phone subtoolbar.
6. **Large Manhwa Pack Opening Performance (`1-30` chapters / 100–400 MB PDFs & CBZs):**
   - **Zero duplicate file reads:** `openBook()` in `src/context/LibraryContext.tsx` passes the resolved `Blob` directly to `ReaderShell` via `activeBookFile` (and opens `ReaderShell` immediately for Android SAF `nativeFileUri` files so the user gets instant feedback and streams once instead of twice).
   - **Zero-copy PDF streaming:** `openPdfFromBlobReliably()` in `src/parsers/pdfParser.ts` uses `URL.createObjectURL(blob)` with `disableAutoFetch: true` instead of `blob.arrayBuffer()` + `data.slice()` (which previously copied 200+ MB 2–3× into RAM).
   - **GPU-safe Webtoon canvas clamping & shared observer:** `src/components/readers/PdfReader.tsx` uses a single shared `IntersectionObserver`, pre-groups annotations by page, skips `TextLayer` extraction for scanned visual series, releases offscreen canvas backing stores (`1x1`), and clamps tall webtoon canvas dimensions (`<= 8192px`, pixel budget capped) so tall strips never exceed GPU texture limits.
   - **Lazy on-demand CBZ extraction:** `openCbzArchive()` in `src/parsers/comicParser.ts` + `LazyComicStripSlot` in `src/components/readers/ComicMangaReader.tsx` index the ZIP directory (~20 ms) and extract images on demand with a 28-page LRU Object URL cache.
   - **Retroactive & filename-first series parsing:** `parseChapterPackInfo()` evaluates both `fileName` and `title` (so embedded PDF titles or hyphen splits never break grouping), handles leading/trailing/mid ranges (`1-30`, `del 1 al 30`, `1 a 30`, trailing single chapter numbers), strips scanlation/language/ending noise (`FINAL`, `FIN`, `+ Extras`, `Epílogo`), performs fuzzy/prefix/folder bucket merging (`areSeriesKeysCompatible`), and retroactively groups split chapter-pack PDFs imported previously as `kind: 'book'`.
7. **Full-Series Actions, First-Image Cover & Auto-Attaching Ongoing Chapters:**
   - **Full-series mark-read/reset/delete:** `BookCard.tsx` passes `seriesMeta?.seriesKey` to `openBookOptionsMenu()`, so `src/components/BookOptionsSheet.tsx` and `src/components/MangaSeriesModal.tsx` update ALL chapter packs in `series.allBooks` via `updateSeriesBooks()` (never just `primaryBook`).
   - **First-chapter crisp top cover:** `extractPdfMetaAndCover()` in `src/parsers/pdfParser.ts` renders Page 1 at full 420px cover width cropped to 2:3 (`<= 1.55x` aspect) so tall webtoon pages never shrink to slivers, and `buildSeriesGroup()` always prioritizes `chapters[0].book` as the series cover.
   - **Automatic ongoing-series absorption:** `findMatchingExistingSeries()` in `src/utils/mangaSeriesUtils.ts` is called inside both `importFiles()` and `importNativeFolderBooks()` so newly uploaded future chapters automatically attach to their existing series and inherit `seriesName`, `kind`, `originTag`, `genres`, `fansubs`, `seriesStatus`, `totalChaptersExpected`, `synopsis`, `favorite`, and `wishlist`.
8. **Unified Reading Log, Dedicated Manga Section & Non-Invasive Linked Folder (`v1.14.0`):**
   - `src/components/ReadingLogView.tsx` groups Manga/Manhwa/Comic chapter packs via `groupLibraryForDisplay()` so a 100+ chapter series appears as ONE unified title whose status, rating, review, and favorite propagate across all packs via `updateSeriesBooks()`.
   - Dedicated `navSection: 'manga'` in `src/components/Sidebar.tsx` and `src/components/BookShelf.tsx` shows exclusively Manga/Manhwa/Manhua/Comic series with origin pills, while `home` continues displaying the full library.
   - Per-device linked folder (`LinkedFolderConfig` in local IndexedDB `kv`) works natively on Electron (`komo:pickWatchFolder`, `komo:scanWatchFolder`, `komo:readWatchFile` in `electron/main.cjs`) and Android SAF (`rescanLibraryFolder` in `android/app/src/main/java/com/komolib/reader/MainActivity.java`) — skipping already-imported files before disk reads and checking at most once on startup (5s idle delay + 15-min cooldown, zero `setInterval` polling).
9. **AI Enrichment Counter & Full Visual Series Completion (`v1.14.1`):**
   - `BookItem.aiEnrichedAt` is stamped on every valid `/api/ai/identify-book` response so `bookNeedsMetadataEnrichment()` in `src/utils/bookMetadata.ts` genuinely decrements the `Completar con IA (N)` badge instead of looping on items without a known author.
   - `/api/ai/identify-book` in both `cloudflare/src/index.js` and `server/index.js` now attaches multimodal `coverDataUrl`, sets `thinkingBudget: 0`, checks `gRes.ok`, and returns full Manga/Manhwa/Comic ficha metadata (`seriesName`, `originTag`, `genres`, `seriesStatus`, `totalChaptersExpected`, `description`), which `identifyBookWithAI` propagates to every book in the series via `updateSeriesBooks()`.
   - `cleanSeriesNoise()` also strips `Tomo N`, `Vol. N`, `Pack N`, `Parte N`, `T1/S1`, and `hasMultiPacks` promotes numbered multi-PDF manhwas (`Nombre 01.pdf`, `Nombre 02.pdf`) so every series unifies reliably.
10. **Multi-Translator / Multi-Fansub Splitting, Dual Titles, Extras, Manual Merge & Zero-Quota AI Catalog Fallback (`v1.14.2`):**
    - Spanish scanlations frequently change translation teams mid-series (e.g., `Olympus Scan`, `Meian Scans`, `Traducciones Amistosas`, `Mango Scan`, `Ajce Translations`). `parseSingleSource()` normalizes underscore-separated ranges (`_01_10`) and underscores BEFORE regex matching, strips both leading and trailing recognized fansubs (`stripUnbracketedFansubPrefix` / `stripUnbracketedFansubSuffix` — NEVER chopping normal Spanish titles containing `"por"` like *La Villana es Adorada por la Familia*), extracts parenthesized/slash alternative titles (`Mi Razón Para Morir (My Reason to Die)`, `Equilibrio Térmico / Heat Balance`) into `altKeys` so Spanish and English packs unify automatically without polluting `fansubs[]`, separates `isExtra` packs (`Extras 1–12`) in deduplication/sorting so they never collide with `Caps. 1–12`, and cleans stored `b.seriesName` consistently (`allowBareTrailingSingle = false`) with self-healing for previously truncated titles.
    - `parseTelegramSeriesCard()` supports all popular Spanish Telegram card templates (`:`, `➳`, markdown `*Title*`, `𝗦𝗜𝗣𝗡𝗢𝗦𝗜𝗦:`, multi-line genres, space-separated hashtags, `(93/93)+(12/12)` sum totals, `Manhwa:` header keys, and chapter counts embedded inside `Estado: Finalizado (98/98)`).
    - `MangaSeriesModal.tsx` adds a 1-click **"Unir capítulos / series"** picker so users can also manually absorb any split packs or alternate-title files into one unified series (setting `seriesNameLocked: true`, syncing PC ↔ Android).
11. **Filename-First Spanish Grouping & Seamless Next-Pack Auto-Advance (`v1.14.2`):**
    - Android SAF PDF metadata (`MainActivity.java` `inspectPdfFile` `/Title`) often contains English/Korean/converter titles (`The Greatest Estate Developer`, `ilovepdf_merged`) that previously overwrote Spanish filenames inside `importNativeFolderBooks()` and poisoned synced `seriesName` values.
    - Both `importNativeFolderBooks()` (`src/context/LibraryContext.tsx`) and `parseChapterPackInfo()` / `groupLibraryForDisplay()` (`src/utils/mangaSeriesUtils.ts`) now treat **`fileName` as the ground truth** for all visual formats unless `BookItem.seriesNameLocked === true`, and automatically group any 2+ PDF/CBZ/CBR/image files sharing the same normalized Spanish name (even if previously imported as `kind: 'book'` or poisoned in cloud sync), while leaving EPUB/MOBI/DOCX/TXT/Audiobooks and standalone PDF books untouched.
    - **Automatic Next-Pack Advance:** `ReaderShell.tsx`, `PdfReader.tsx`, and `ComicMangaReader.tsx` now pass `nextSeriesLabel` / `onOpenNextSeriesPack` so finishing a batch (`1–10`) in either continuous vertical scroll (950 ms smooth bottom countdown with cancel option) or paginated mode (advancing past the last page) marks the batch `100%` completed and jumps straight into the next batch (`11–20`).
12. **AI Content Classification (`book`/`audiobook`/`manga`/`manhwa`/`manhua`/`comic`), Cross-Title Name Unification & Linux `.desktop` Symlink (`v1.14.2`):**
    - `/api/ai/identify-book` (full parity across `cloudflare/src/index.js` and `server/index.js`) now accepts `format`, `currentKind`, `folderPath`, and `existingSeriesNames[]`, returning `detectedKind` and verifying/unifying `seriesName` against existing Spanish series in the library (both via Gemini multimodal prompt and via MangaDex/AniList/Jikan `altTitles` cross-matching when Gemini hits `429`, with strict confidence `titleScore >= 60` on PDFs so prose books are never misclassified).
    - `BookShelf.tsx` adds a **"Clasificar y unir con IA"** action alongside **"Completar con IA"** to classify PDF/CBZ/CBR items and merge split packs with renamed/English titles into their canonical Spanish series (locking `seriesNameLocked: true`), while leaving standard EPUB/MOBI/DOCX/TXT books untouched.
    - `package.json` excludes `node_modules` (`!node_modules/**/*`, `npmRebuild: false`, shrinking `app.asar` from 121 MB → 4.5 MB) and maintains a stable `release/Komolib.AppImage` symlink so Linux KDE `.desktop` launchers never break across version bumps.
    - **Desktop CPU & RAM Optimization:** `groupLibraryForDisplay()` now uses a `WeakMap<BookItem[], ShelfDisplayItem[]>` cache + O(N) inverted token/folder index (eliminating O(N²) regex loops across 400+ books); `electron/main.cjs` enables `backgroundThrottling: true`, drops forced `CanvasOopRasterization` / `enable-gpu-rasterization`, and caps V8 heap with `--max-old-space-size=1024`; Google Drive `focus`/`visibilitychange` listeners now use version-check-only (`checkVersionOnly = true`) with a 45 s cooldown and 60 s poll interval instead of re-downloading multi-MB state JSON on every window focus.
    - `/api/ai/identify-book` (with full parity across `cloudflare/src/index.js` and `server/index.js`) cascades across `gemini-2.5-flash` → `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` on `429`/`503`/`404` (avoiding deprecated `gemini-2.0-flash`); when Gemini free-tier quota hits `429`, it seamlessly falls back to public metadata catalogs with an explicit `User-Agent: KomoLib/1.14` header: **MangaDex** (native Spanish titles & descriptions) → **AniList GraphQL** → **Jikan/MAL** → **Wikipedia ES** for visual series, and **Google Books (`langRestrict=es`)** → **Open Library (+ work details)** → **Wikipedia ES** for books.
    - Enlarged, vertically resizable synopsis fields (`rows={6}`, `minHeight: 124–128px`, `.series-ficha-synopsis p { max-height: 280px }`) in `src/components/BookDetailModal.tsx`, `src/components/MangaSeriesModal.tsx`, and `src/styles/global.css`.
